1. Live Currency Rates, Pip Spreads & Pip Calculators

Access live currency pairs at /Forex/ForexMarket:

  • Major Currency Pairs: Continuous live rate streams for EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, USD/CHF, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, and NZD/USD.
  • Bid/Ask Spread Tracking: Displays live spreads in pips. Alerts you when spreads widen beyond standard thresholds during market rollover (21:00 UTC) or news releases.
  • Pip Calculator Engine: Computes the exact monetary value of 1 pip based on the counter currency and position size:
    • 1.00 Standard Lot (100,000 units) = $10.00 / pip (EUR/USD)
    • 0.10 Mini Lot (10,000 units) = $1.00 / pip
    • 0.01 Micro Lot (1,000 units) = $0.10 / pip

2. Technical Indicators, Session Pivots & Currency Strength

Perform technical analysis at /Forex/ForexAnalysis:

  • Multi-Timeframe Indicators: Exponential Moving Averages (EMA 20, 50, 200), RSI 14, MACD, Bollinger Bands, and Average True Range (ATR 14 in pips).
  • Daily & Session Pivot Points: Automatically calculates Floor Pivots (P, R1-R3, S1-S3) tailored to the London, New York, and Tokyo trading sessions.
  • Relative Currency Strength Matrix: Evaluates the relative performance of individual currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CHF, CAD, AUD, NZD) across all pairs to pair the strongest currency against the weakest for maximum directional momentum.

3. Economic Calendar & Central Bank Macro Analysis

Anticipate market-moving events with the integrated economic calendar:

  • Tier-1 Economic Releases: Categorizes news events by impact level (Red = High, Orange = Medium, Yellow = Low), including US Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP), Consumer Price Index (CPI), and GDP data.
  • Central Bank Policy Surveillance: Tracks interest rate decisions, policy statements, and press conferences from the Federal Reserve (FOMC), European Central Bank (ECB), Bank of England (BoE), and Bank of Japan (BoJ).
  • News Trading Risk Filters: Automatic notifications recommending trading pauses 15 minutes before and after high-impact announcements to prevent extreme slippage.

4. Algorithmic Strategy Engine & Pip Backtester

Backtest quantitative Forex strategies at /Forex/ForexStrategy:

  • Strategy Models: London Breakout (session high/low breakout), Moving Average Pullback (EMA 50/200 trend alignment with RSI dips), and Mean Reversion (Bollinger Band extremes).
  • Spread & Commission Modeling: Injects realistic broker spreads (1.2 to 2.5 pips) and round-turn lot commissions ($5.00/lot) into backtest simulations to ensure true net profitability.
  • Institutional Metrics: Total Net Pip Gain, Win Rate %, Profit Factor, Maximum Drawdown (in % and pips), and Sharpe Ratio.

5. Order Execution, Lot Sizing & Paper Trading

Place and manage FX positions at /Forex/ForexTrade:

  • Order Types: Market Execution (instant fill), Limit Entry (Buy Limit / Sell Limit), and Stop Entry (Buy Stop / Sell Stop).
  • Automated Lot Sizing: Calculates allowable lots using the formula:
    Lots = (Account Balance × Risk %) / (Stop Loss in Pips × Pip Value)
  • Paper Trading Simulator: Test order entries, trailing stops, and multi-tier take-profits with zero financial exposure before executing on live broker accounts.

6. Margin Control, Regulatory Leverage & Emergency Kill Switch

Enforce strict risk management at /Forex/ForexRisk:

  • European ESMA / Dutch AFM Retail Leverage Caps: Configured for maximum regulatory limits: 30:1 for major currency pairs, 20:1 for non-major pairs and gold, and 10:1 for other commodities.
  • Margin Level Monitoring: Real-time margin level percentage tracking with warning thresholds at 100% margin and stop-out liquidation protections at 50%.
  • Daily Loss Limit: Shuts down trade execution when cumulative daily losses breach user-defined parameters (e.g. 2.5% max daily drawdown).
  • One-Click Kill Switch: Instantly flattens all open currency positions and cancels all pending limit/stop orders.

7. Performance Trade Journal & Tax Audit Export

Track trading performance at /Forex/ForexJournal:

  • Detailed Journal Log: Records open time, close time, pair, lot size, entry price, exit price, pip gain/loss, swap fees, and gross/net dollar P&L.
  • Performance Breakdown: Analyzes win rate and average risk-to-reward ratio grouped by individual currency pair and trading session (London vs New York).
  • Tax Report Export: Exports audit-ready CSV and PDF records summarizing net realized gains and deductible broker commissions for annual financial declarations.

8. Broker Integration & Operational Safety Checklist

Configure broker connectivity at /Forex/ForexSettings:

  • Broker Connectors: Connect via REST or FIX APIs to supported brokers (e.g., OANDA, MetaTrader 4/5 bridges, Interactive Brokers).
  • Environment Toggles: Switch seamlessly between Demo / Sandbox environments and Live accounts.
  • Overnight Financing (Swap): Displays positive and negative rollover swap rates to prevent unexpected holding costs on multi-day positions.
