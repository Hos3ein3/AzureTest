1. Live Market Data, Order Depth & Perpetuals Hub

Access aggregated crypto markets at /Crypto/CryptoMarket:

  • Multi-Source Price Aggregation: Pulls real-time prices, 24h volume, price changes, and market capitalization across top cryptocurrency pairs from CoinGecko, CoinLore, DIA, Kraken, and Binance public endpoints.
  • Candlestick Charts (/Crypto/CryptoMarket/Chart): Inspect multi-timeframe candles (1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h, 1d) with interactive volume histograms.
  • Level 2 Order Book & Tape (/Crypto/CryptoMarket/OrderBook): Inspect real-time bid/ask depth distributions, spread percentages, and live transaction prints.
  • Perpetual Futures & Funding Rates (/Crypto/CryptoMarket/Futures): Monitor 8-hour funding rates, annualized APR, and Open Interest (OI) metrics to gauge market leverage sentiment and squeeze risks.

2. Technical Indicators, Support/Resistance & Fibonacci

Explore technical analysis at /Crypto/CryptoAnalysis:

  • Moving Averages: Fast and slow trends with EMA 9, EMA 21, EMA 50, EMA 200, and SMA 20/50.
  • Oscillators & Volatility: 14-period RSI with dynamic overbought (≥ 70) and oversold (≤ 30) alerts, MACD (12, 26, 9) signal lines and histograms, Bollinger Bands (20, 2.0 std dev), and Average True Range (ATR 14).
  • Classical Floor Pivots: Automatic calculation of Daily Pivot (P), Resistances (R1, R2, R3), and Supports (S1, S2, S3).
  • Fibonacci Retracement Grid: High-precision Fibonacci levels (23.6%, 38.2%, 50.0% Equilibrium, 61.8% Golden Pocket, 78.6% Deep Pullback) mapped to recent price swings.

3. Market Sentiment, On-Chain Whales & Screener

Assess macro drivers and uncover opportunities with dedicated analytical tools:

  • Crypto Sentiment (/Crypto/CryptoAnalysis/Sentiment): Alternative.me Crypto Fear & Greed index gauge, BTC dominance ratio, and live curated news feeds with bullish/bearish classification from CryptoPanic.
  • On-Chain Whale Radar (/Crypto/CryptoAnalysis/OnChain): Live blockchain explorer feeds detecting large wallet transfers, exchange inflows (potential sell pressure), and exchange outflows (cold storage accumulation).
  • Quantitative Screener (/Crypto/CryptoAnalysis/Screener): Multi-variable screener identifying coins by 24h trading volume surges, ATR volatility expansion, and RSI momentum.

4. Algorithmic Strategies & Walk-Forward Backtesting

Design and validate trading rules at /Crypto/CryptoStrategy:

  • Rule-Based Archetypes: Trend-Following (EMA crosses + ATR filters), Mean-Reversion (RSI oversold + lower Bollinger bounces), and Volatility Breakout (Bollinger squeeze breakouts).
  • Realistic Backtest Engine: Configures realistic taker/maker trading fees (0.10%) and dynamic execution slippage (0.05%) to prevent unrealistic profit projections.
  • Performance Reports: Evaluates Win Rate %, Profit Factor, Max Drawdown %, and Annualized Sharpe Ratio with cumulative equity curves.
  • Walk-Forward Cross Validation: Splits historical candles into 60% In-Sample (training) and 40% Out-of-Sample (validation). Calculates parameter stability and flags curve-fitted strategies before live deployment.

5. Position Sizing & Institutional Risk Controls

Protect your capital at /Crypto/CryptoRisk:

  • Formulaic Position Sizing: Automatically computes order quantity based on account balance, target risk percentage per trade (e.g. 1.0% – 2.0%), and stop-loss distance.
  • Strict Trade Exits: Mandates Stop-Loss, Take-Profit, and dynamic Trailing Stop rules on every trade before execution.
  • Daily Drawdown Circuit Breaker: Locks out new order submissions if daily losses exceed the configured limit (e.g. 3.0% daily cap).
  • Emergency Kill Switch: Instantly cancels all resting limit orders and liquidates all open positions into base currency with a single click.

6. Order Execution & Paper-to-Live Migration

Execute spot and perpetual orders at /Crypto/CryptoTrade:

  • Paper Trading Mode by Default: All accounts initialize in risk-free Paper Trading simulation mode with simulated cash balances.
  • Order Types: Market orders (immediate fill at market price), Limit orders (maker queue entry), and Stop-Market protective orders.
  • Idempotent Safe Retries: Every order generates a unique client idempotency key to prevent accidental duplicate fills during network timeouts.
  • Staged Migration Protocol: Live trading is strictly locked until you complete 50 paper trades and verify out-of-sample strategy robustness.

7. Portfolio Management, Trade Journal & Tax Export

Monitor assets and maintain audit trails at /Crypto/CryptoPortfolio:

  • Portfolio Overview: Asset balances, open position monitor, unrealized P&L, and total account equity.
  • Structured Trade Journal (/Crypto/CryptoPortfolio/Journal): Record strategy rationale, setup tags, entry/exit screenshots, and psychological ratings for each trade.
  • Tax Report Exporter (/Crypto/CryptoPortfolio/TaxReport): Generates fiscal summaries with realized gains, transaction fees, and asset valuations on January 1st for Dutch Belastingdienst Box 3 filing.

8. Real-Time Alerts & API Key Vault Security

Automate monitoring and protect credentials at /Crypto/CryptoAlerts and /Crypto/CryptoSettings:

  • Trigger Alerts: Create notifications for price crossings, RSI overbought/oversold levels, MACD crosses, and large whale transactions.
  • Encrypted Key Vault: Exchange API keys and secrets are encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • Zero Withdrawal Privilege Enforcement: The platform explicitly rejects any exchange API keys that have withdrawal permissions enabled.

9. Risk Warning & Regulatory Disclaimers

Not Financial Advice: This software is an algorithmic trading tool and quantitative analytics dashboard. Cryptocurrency markets are highly volatile.

  • Leverage & Liquidation Cascades: Leveraged positions risk 100% loss of allocated margin. Cascading liquidations on centralized exchanges can cause catastrophic slippage during flash crashes.
  • Free Data Delay Latency: Free public API endpoints (CoinGecko, Binance REST) experience polling delays of 1 to 60 seconds compared to collocated HFT WebSockets. Never execute high-frequency scalping strategies on delayed public feeds.
  • EU MiCA & Dutch AFM Compliance: The EU Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) regulation and Dutch AFM enforce strict retail investor protections and restrictions on high-leverage derivatives. Operating algorithms with third-party funds requires MiFID II licensing.
